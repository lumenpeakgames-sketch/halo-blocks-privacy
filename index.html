PRIVACY POLICY FOR HALO BLOCKS Effective date: September 5, 2026 Lumen Peak Games ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile game Halo Blocks (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, processed, and shared when you use Halo Blocks. By using the Game, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. 1. INFORMATION WE PROCESS Halo Blocks does not require you to create a separate account with Lumen Peak Games. We do not directly request or collect information such as: - your full name; - postal address; - telephone number; - contacts; - photographs; - precise GPS location. However, some information may be processed automatically by third-party services integrated into the Game, including Google Mobile Ads and Google Play Games Services. 2. GAME DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE Halo Blocks may store gameplay-related information locally on your device, including: - high scores; - virtual coin balance; - unlocked or purchased cosmetic items; - selected blocks, themes, and avatars; - game settings; - daily challenge progress; - other gameplay progression and preferences. This information is primarily used to provide and maintain the Game's functionality. Local gameplay data may be removed if you clear the Game's application data or uninstall the Game, subject to any information separately stored by third-party services such as Google Play Games. 3. GOOGLE ADMOB AND ADVERTISING Halo Blocks uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements. Rewarded advertisements are optional advertisements that may provide an in-game benefit after the advertisement has been successfully completed. Examples may include: - continuing a game after losing; - receiving additional virtual coins. Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and process information such as: - IP address, which may be used to estimate general location; - advertising identifiers and other device or account identifiers; - information about interactions with advertisements and the Game; - advertisement views and video interactions; - diagnostic and performance information; - device and application information. This information may be used for purposes including: - delivering advertisements; - measuring advertisement performance; - analytics; - preventing fraud and abuse; - improving advertising services. Depending on your location and privacy choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized. Google states that information transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. For more information about how Google processes information, please see Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy You can also learn more about Google's advertising technologies at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads 4. CONSENT AND PRIVACY CHOICES Halo Blocks uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent requirements where applicable. Advertising requests are made in accordance with the consent status made available through Google's consent system and applicable legal requirements. Where required, users may later access privacy options again through functionality provided in the Game. 5. GOOGLE PLAY GAMES SERVICES Halo Blocks may use Google Play Games Services for features such as: - player authentication; - leaderboards; - high scores; - achievements; - player names; - player profile images or avatars. When you use Google Play Games features, Google may process information related to your Google Play Games profile and game activity. This may include: - your gamer identity; - gamer name; - profile image; - game scores; - leaderboard rankings; - achievements; - game activity; - device and performance information. Halo Blocks may send gameplay information such as scores and achievements to Google Play Games in order to provide leaderboard and achievement functionality. Google Play Games data is handled by Google according to Google's own privacy policies and your Google Play Games privacy settings. For more information: https://policies.google.com/privacy You can manage Google Play Games profile and privacy settings through your Google account and Google Play Games settings. 6.
