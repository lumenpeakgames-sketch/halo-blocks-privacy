Lumen Peak Games - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Lumen Peak Games ("we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes mobile games. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, processed, and shared when you use a Lumen Peak Games title that links to this Privacy Policy (the "Game" or collectively the "Games").

This Privacy Policy currently applies to games including Halo Blocks and Crash Circuit. It may also apply to future Lumen Peak Games titles when those titles link to this policy.

1. Information We Process

Our Games generally do not require you to create a separate Lumen Peak Games account.

We do not directly request information such as your full name, postal address, telephone number, contacts, photographs, or precise GPS location.

Depending on the Game and the features you use, certain information may be processed automatically by third-party services integrated into the Game, such as Google Mobile Ads, Google User Messaging Platform, Google Play Games Services, and Google Play Billing.

2. Game Data Stored on Your Device

Our Games may store gameplay-related information locally on your device, including high scores, virtual currency balances, unlocked or purchased cosmetic items, selected characters, skins, themes, boards, blocks or avatars, game settings, daily challenge or reward progress, missions, achievements, statistics, and other gameplay progression.

This information is primarily used to provide and maintain the Game's functionality. Local gameplay data may be removed if you clear the Game's application data or uninstall the Game, subject to information that may separately be stored or restored through third-party services such as Google Play.

3. Advertising

Some Lumen Peak Games titles use Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements. Depending on the Game, these may include rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements.

Rewarded advertisements are optional advertisements that may provide an in-game benefit after successful completion, for example continuing a run after losing, receiving additional virtual currency, or increasing a gameplay reward.

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and process information such as IP address, advertising identifiers and other device or account identifiers, interactions with advertisements and the Game, ad views and video interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and device and application information.

This information may be used to deliver advertisements, measure performance, provide analytics, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve advertising services. Depending on your location and privacy choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

Games that use Google advertising may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent requirements where applicable. Users in jurisdictions where consent or privacy choices are required may be shown a privacy message allowing them to provide consent, decline consent, or manage available privacy choices.

Advertising requests are made in accordance with the consent status made available through Google's consent system and applicable legal requirements. Where required, privacy options may be made available again through the Game's settings.

5. In-App Purchases and Google Play Billing

Some Lumen Peak Games titles, including Crash Circuit, may offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play. Purchases may include virtual currency, cosmetic content, bundles, or features such as removing automatic advertisements.

Payments are processed by Google Play. Lumen Peak Games does not directly receive or store your full payment card details.

Google may process information related to transactions, purchase status, purchase tokens, Google account information, device information, and fraud-prevention signals in accordance with Google's own policies and Google Play terms.

The Game may locally store purchase entitlements or transaction identifiers where necessary to restore purchases, prevent duplicate grants, and maintain purchased content.

6. Google Play Games Services

Some Games, including Halo Blocks where applicable, may use Google Play Games Services for features such as player authentication, leaderboards, high scores, achievements, player names, and profile images or avatars.

When you use Google Play Games features, Google may process information related to your Google Play Games profile and game activity, including gamer identity, gamer name, profile image, scores, rankings, achievements, game activity, and device or performance information.

7. Analytics

Lumen Peak Games does not currently operate a separate proprietary analytics platform for the Games covered by this policy. Third-party services used by a Game may process usage, diagnostic, advertising, transaction, security, and performance information for their own service operation, analytics, fraud-prevention, and security purposes.

If we introduce additional analytics services in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

8. How Information Is Used

Information processed through our Games and integrated services may be used to provide and operate the Games, save gameplay progress and preferences, provide leaderboards and achievements where available, process or restore in-app purchases, display advertisements, provide rewarded advertising features, respect privacy choices, maintain security, detect fraud or abuse, diagnose technical problems, and improve stability and performance.

9. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information for monetary compensation.

Information may be processed or shared with third-party service providers when necessary to provide features of a Game. Depending on the Game, providers may include Google LLC through Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google User Messaging Platform, Google Play Games Services, and Google Play Billing / Google Play services.

10. Personalized Advertising

Depending on your location, applicable law, Google configuration, and your privacy choices, Google AdMob may serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements. Where consent is legally required, privacy choices may be collected through Google's User Messaging Platform.

You may also be able to manage advertising preferences through your Google account and Android device settings.

11. Children's Privacy

The intended audience and age suitability may vary by Game and are described in the relevant store listing.

We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Third-party services integrated into our Games may apply their own age-related requirements, privacy practices, and advertising restrictions.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information directly to Lumen Peak Games, please contact us using the details below.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the operation of our Games. Information transmitted to third-party services is protected according to the security practices of those providers. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Data Retention

Gameplay data stored locally by a Game remains on your device for as long as necessary to provide the Game's functionality or until it is removed. You can generally remove locally stored data by clearing the application's data through Android settings or uninstalling the Game.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to their own retention policies and your account settings.

We do not currently maintain a separate Lumen Peak Games user-account database for Halo Blocks or Crash Circuit.

14. Data Deletion

Because the Games currently covered by this policy do not provide separate Lumen Peak Games user accounts, there is no separate Lumen Peak Games game account that must be deleted through our servers.

You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the Game. Information stored through Google services may need to be managed through your Google account, Google Play, Google Play Games, or other Google privacy controls.

If you have a privacy or deletion request relating specifically to information controlled by Lumen Peak Games, contact us using the details below.

15. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to access information, request correction or deletion, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and manage advertising and privacy preferences.

Some information used by our Games is processed directly by third-party service providers. Requests concerning information controlled by those providers may need to be submitted through their own privacy and account-management tools.

16. European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

For users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, or other jurisdictions where consent is required, Games using Google advertising may use Google's User Messaging Platform to obtain and manage applicable advertising consent choices.

Where required, personalized advertising will not be requested unless applicable consent requirements have been satisfied. Users may be given the opportunity to manage or change their privacy choices.

17. United States Privacy Rights

Residents of certain U.S. states may have additional privacy rights under applicable state privacy laws. Depending on applicable law, these rights may include the right to know what personal information is processed, request access or deletion, correct inaccurate information, or opt out of certain forms of targeted advertising, sharing, or sale.

Lumen Peak Games does not sell personal information for monetary compensation. Advertising providers such as Google may process information for advertising purposes in accordance with user privacy choices and applicable law.

18. International Data Processing

Third-party service providers used by our Games may process information in countries other than the country in which you live. Such processing is subject to the privacy and data-transfer safeguards implemented by those service providers and applicable law.

19. Links and Third-Party Services

Our Games may interact with third-party services such as Google Play, Google Play Games, Google advertising services, and Google Play Billing. We are not responsible for the independent privacy practices of third-party services and encourage you to review their privacy policies.
