At a glance

  • You don't need a Lumen Peak Games account to play our games.
  • We don't ask for your name, address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Game progress and settings are stored on your device.
  • Some games show ads through Google AdMob, and some offer optional purchases through Google Play.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can contact us at any time with a privacy question or request.

1Who we are and scope

Lumen Peak Games ("we", "us" or "our") develops and publishes mobile games. This Privacy Policy applies to every game published by Lumen Peak Games that links to this page (each a "Game", together the "Games"). This currently includes Halo Blocks, Duckdoku, Capy Days, Crash Circuit and Capy Wheels, and it will also apply to future Lumen Peak Games titles that link to this page.

Our Games don't all use the same features. When a section below only applies to some Games, it says so. Section 17 lists the third-party services each Game uses.

2Information you provide

Our Games do not require you to create a Lumen Peak Games account, and they don't ask you to enter personal information such as your name, email address, postal address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise GPS location.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to answer you and to deal with your request.

3Game data stored on your device

Our Games may store gameplay information locally on your device so that they work as expected. Depending on the Game, this can include:

  • scores, high scores, levels and other progress;
  • in-game currency balances and unlocked or purchased items (for example characters, skins, themes or vehicles);
  • game settings such as sound, music and vibration preferences;
  • daily rewards, challenges, missions, achievements and statistics;
  • records needed to keep or restore in-app purchases.

We do not send this local game data to servers operated by Lumen Peak Games. It is removed when you clear the Game's app data or uninstall the Game, except for information that Google services store separately (see Section 7).

4Information collected automatically

Lumen Peak Games does not run its own servers that collect information from the Games. However, third-party services built into some Games may collect certain information automatically when you play. Depending on the Game and the features you use, this may include:

  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, app version and settings;
  • online identifiers, such as IP address, the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • interactions with ads, such as ad views, clicks and completed rewarded videos;
  • purchase information, such as purchase status and purchase tokens, when you buy something in a Game;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash logs and performance data.

These services handle this information under their own privacy policies, described in the sections below.

5Advertising

Some of our Games are free to play and show ads to support development. Depending on the Game, these can include:

  • Rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for an in-game benefit, such as continuing a run, getting more in-game currency or doubling a reward;
  • Interstitial ads, which are full-screen ads shown at natural breaks in gameplay.

Depending on where you live, applicable law and your privacy choices, the ads you see may be personalized or non-personalized.

Consent and privacy choices

Games that show ads use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Where required, you will see a privacy message where you can give consent, decline it or choose which options to allow. Personalized ads are not requested unless these consent requirements are met. Where the law requires it, you can review or change your choices later from the Game's settings.

Managing ads on your device

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads (menu names vary by device). You can also manage ad personalization in your Google account settings.

6Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads

Games that show ads use Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, provided by Google LLC. Google may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • the advertising ID, app set ID and other device identifiers;
  • interactions with ads and with the Game;
  • diagnostic, performance and device information.

Google uses this information to show ads, measure ad performance, provide ad analytics, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve its services. To learn more, see:

Google Privacy Policy How Google uses information for advertising How Google uses information from apps that use its services

7Google Play Services

Our Games are distributed through Google Play. Google's own privacy policy and terms apply to the Google Play Store, your Google account and the Google Play services on your device.

Google Play Games Services

Some Games use Google Play Games Services for features such as signing in with your Play Games profile, leaderboards and achievements. When you use these features, Google processes information linked to your Play Games profile, such as your gamer ID, player name, profile image, scores, rankings and achievements. Your player name and scores may be visible to other players on leaderboards, depending on your Play Games profile settings. You can manage this information in the Google Play Games app and in your Google account.

Google Play Billing

Games that offer in-app purchases use Google Play Billing. See Section 9.

Statistics from Google Play

Google Play gives developers aggregated statistics about their apps, such as installs, ratings, and crash and performance reports (Android vitals). We use this information only to understand how our Games perform and to fix problems. It does not identify you to us.

8Analytics and crash reporting

We do not currently use a separate analytics or crash-reporting service (such as Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics) in our Games. We do not build profiles about you.

We use the aggregated statistics and crash reports that Google Play provides (see Section 7), as well as the ad reports that Google AdMob provides for Games that show ads. These reports are aggregated and do not identify individual players to us.

If we add an analytics or crash-reporting service in the future, we will update this policy before or when that change takes effect.

9In-app purchases

Some Games offer optional in-app purchases, such as in-game currency, cosmetic items, bundles or removing ads. You can play the Games without buying anything.

All payments are processed by Google Play. Lumen Peak Games never receives or stores your payment card details. Google processes information related to the transaction, such as your Google account, purchase status, purchase tokens, device information and fraud-prevention signals, under its own policies and the Google Play terms.

A Game may store purchase entitlements or transaction identifiers on your device so that it can deliver what you bought, avoid granting the same purchase twice and restore purchases. For refunds and purchase history, use Google Play.

10How we use information

Information handled by our Games and the services built into them is used to:

  • run the Games and save your progress, items and settings;
  • provide leaderboards and achievements where available;
  • process, deliver and restore in-app purchases;
  • show ads and provide rewarded ad features;
  • respect your consent and privacy choices;
  • keep the Games secure and prevent fraud or abuse;
  • find and fix bugs, crashes and performance problems;
  • reply to your messages and requests;
  • comply with legal obligations.

11Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your personal information.

Information is shared with, or collected directly by, third-party service providers only where it is needed for features of a Game. The third-party services currently used across our Games are all provided by Google LLC:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK (ads);
  • Google User Messaging Platform (consent management);
  • Google Play Games Services (leaderboards and achievements);
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases).

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or the safety of our users, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets. In that case, this policy or an equivalent one will continue to apply.

12Data retention and deletion

Retention

Game data stored on your device remains there until you clear the Game's app data or uninstall the Game. Lumen Peak Games does not keep a user-account database for any of the Games. We keep emails you send us only as long as needed to handle your request, and then delete them.

Third-party services such as Google keep information under their own retention policies and your account settings.

Deletion

Because our Games don't use Lumen Peak Games accounts, there is no account on our side to delete. You can delete your data as follows:

  • Local game data: on Android, go to Settings → Apps → [Game] → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the Game.
  • Google Play Games data (leaderboards, achievements): manage or delete it in the Google Play Games app or your Google account.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your device's privacy settings.
  • Emails you sent us: email us and ask us to delete them.

13Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information related to our Games. We keep the data we handle to a minimum. The third-party services we use encrypt data in transit and apply their own security practices. However, no method of transmitting or storing data is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14Children's privacy

The intended audience and age rating of each Game are shown on its store listing. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If a Game is aimed at children or a mixed audience including children, we configure the services in that Game as Google Play's Families policy and applicable laws (such as COPPA and the GDPR) require. For example, ad requests are marked as directed to children, personalized ads are not shown to children, and only Google-certified ad SDKs are used.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

15Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate information corrected;
  • have your information deleted;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent;
  • receive your information in a portable format;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, consent for personalized ads is collected through Google's User Messaging Platform, and you can withdraw or change it where the Game offers that option. Our other processing is based on our legitimate interest in running, securing and improving the Games, or on what is needed to provide the features you use.

United States

Residents of some U.S. states have additional rights under state privacy laws, such as the right to know, delete or correct personal information, and to opt out of targeted advertising or the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell personal information for money. You can limit ad personalization through your device and Google account settings, as described in Section 5.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us. Because much of the data is handled directly by Google, some requests are best made through Google's own privacy tools, and we will point you to them where that applies. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

16International processing

The third-party services used in our Games may process information in countries other than the one you live in, including the United States. These providers use safeguards required by law for such transfers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
